# Demo Mode Verification

The desktop workspace renders the Paper Field Notes calendar shell with the academic date grid, selected-day margin, multiple-account navigation, sync center, and Gemini Spark panel. The post-review visual refinement shifts the rail toward a quieter notebook-index treatment and extends ruled-paper/margin-line motifs into the hero and calendar composition.

At 375 × 812 pixels, the application collapses into a single-column mobile calendar. The header, event action, month controls, date grid, selected-day records, and current-month list remain visible without clipping. The dense calendar cells retain legible dates and event dots, while account and sync workflows remain available from the desktop-equivalent section controls when viewed in the application.

The app intentionally states that Google identity linking is not active without app-owner OAuth credentials. Local account creation and synchronization actions are therefore labeled as demonstration actions and do not claim to access Google data.
