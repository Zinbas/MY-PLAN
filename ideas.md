# Chosen Direction: Paper Field Notes

## Design Movement

Contemporary editorial stationery with the clarity of a field journal and the restraint of Swiss information design.

## Core Principles

The interface uses generous whitespace, a clear reading order, quiet paper-like surfaces, and only functional color. Every event state is communicated through a small marker or thin accent rather than heavy panels or visual noise.

## Color Philosophy

Warm ivory creates a calm study surface, charcoal provides dependable text contrast, terracotta marks high-stakes academic dates, muted olive denotes recovery or holiday periods, and desaturated blue supports neutral planning context. Color is reserved for meaning, never decoration.

## Layout Paradigm

The desktop view uses an asymmetric calendar-and-agenda composition: the calendar occupies the working field while the selected day and event legend form a narrow editorial margin. On mobile, the calendar becomes a focused month view with a collapsible event strip beneath it.

## Signature Elements

Thin charcoal rules establish hierarchy, rounded paper cards hold interactive controls, and small colored dots denote date states. A subtle calendar watermark anchors the page without competing with the dates.

## Interaction Philosophy

Month navigation should feel immediate and deliberate. Selecting an event date updates the adjacent agenda panel, while quick month chips let students jump across the semester with one tap.

## Animation

Month changes use a short opacity-and-translate transition under 220ms. Date selection uses a restrained background and border transition. Motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.

## Typography System

Fraunces is used sparingly for the calendar title and month heading to add editorial character. DM Sans is used for all dates, controls, and event copy to remain highly readable at compact sizes.

## Brand Essence

An academic calendar for students who want a focused, calm view of the semester’s critical dates; clear rather than crowded. Personality: **disciplined, calm, precise**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and factual, while microcopy is supportive but unsentimental. Example lines: “The semester, in one view.” and “Select a marked date to see what matters.” Generic welcome language is avoided.

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is an abstract stacked calendar tab: three offset rounded rectangles with a small terracotta date dot, rendered without text. The wordmark uses the Fraunces display style rather than a default system face.

## Signature Brand Color

Terracotta #C75B43 is the ownable accent used for assignments, quizzes, examinations, and critical dates.

## Style Decisions

Terracotta is reserved for critical-date meaning, key numerals, and fine accents rather than broad decoration. Selected calendar dates and active months use paper tint, a charcoal rule, and thin terracotta markers instead of dense dark fills. Decorative motifs use ruled paper, calendar tabs, and archival stationery marks rather than lifestyle imagery.
