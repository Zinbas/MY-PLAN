# Daily Quote and Onboarding Refinement Notes

MY PLAN’s daily quote should reinforce specific, sustainable planning behaviours rather than imply that constant output is required. The quote set will therefore use original, concise lines about choosing one next action, making a practical plan, adapting when circumstances change, finishing small steps, and taking recovery seriously. This avoids unattributed quote aggregation while fitting both student and professional contexts.

The evidence review supports a planning-forward, low-pressure tone. A field experiment on weekly planning found benefits associated with goal setting, planning work steps, anticipating obstacles, and developing alternatives; it reported lower unfinished tasks and rumination alongside greater cognitive flexibility.[1] A workplace self-management app study also highlights personalization, self-management, and coping supports as more useful than generic pressure messaging for people managing work demands and stress.[2]

The app will determine the same quote for all renders of a given local calendar day, rotating deterministically through the curated set. Welcome will be treated as a first-run experience: a complete tutorial or a signed-in state retires it and routes the user to Calendar instead.
